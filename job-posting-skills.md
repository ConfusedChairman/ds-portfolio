# Job Posting Skills
## Sr. Business Analyst: *Senior Associate, Business*
* Required
  * 2 years experience 
  * Bachelor's or Master's Degree in a quantitative field 
  * Strategic problem solving and analytical thinking (listed as a specific responsibility, analyses and tools development) 
  * Communication and collaboration (frequent teaming up with professionals and senior leadership, interaction with team of diverse roles, partnering with clients, listed as a “successful team member” competency and is likely tested in hiring process thus required, includes visualization skills as well) 
  * Professionalism and reliability (mentions many “high impact” opportunities and “important goals,” involving “strong organizational skills”) 
  * Develop and test hypotheses (Listed as another “successful team member” competency, seems more like a responsibility and expected skill rather than a “nice-to-have" due to its specificity) 
  * Quick adaptability (mentions “rapid prototyping” as a responsibility, requiring quick turnover for tasks and ability to adapt to situations) 
* Preferred
  * Master’s degree in Business or quantitative field 
  * 1+ years experience of Python, SQL querying, consulting 
  * 2+ years experience of business analysis 
## Quantitative Researcher: *Seniority N/A, Investment/Finance*
* Required
  * Collaboration, communication with other teams 
  * Degree in quantitative discipline 
  * Expertise with statistical inference, machine learning, data science, high-dimensional datasets 
  * Fluency with Python, C++, R, or Julia 
  * Ability to test, design experiments and interpret results 
  * Data-driven analytical thinking, problem-solving (related to listed key responsibilities involved in innovation, discovery and evaluation, and entrepreneurship, also connected to required soft skill of “quantitative intuition”) 
  * Intellectual curiosity (listed under required “intrinsics,” implies requirement of staying current and being open to new concepts) 
  * Ability to thrive in fast-paced work environment (listed under required “intrinsics” skills) 
* Preferred
  * Experience with financial markets (implied in relation to “intellectual curiosity,” since it is a highly technical role that involves highly involved economic modeling for financial decisions; it would be difficult to get this job without prior knowledge, and it is a quick-paced work environment) 
  * Ambitiousness, willingness to fail (while key responsibility is being an “intellectual entrepreneur” involving taking new approaches and exploring new questions, does involve a lot of trial-and-error that is implied to involve an ambitious, resilient approach) 
---
### Skills in Both Postings:
* Degree in a quantitative discipline 
* Collaboration and communication skills 
* Designing, testing hypotheses 
* Some level of experience with Python 
* Analytical thinking and problem-solving 
* Quick adaptability 
### Reflection:
I was surprised by how difficult it was for me to find “recommended” skills and attributes. While the Business Analyst role had some prior experience and degrees, it was not very specific beyond that; Technical skills and soft skills were either described implicitly through key responsibilities or hinted through “successful team members” qualities. In this sense, preferred skills blended with an underlying expectation of what an applicant should already exhibit if they were to start the role. With the Quantitative Researcher role, there was only implicit preference through the key responsibilities section. This role was much more succinct and did not really talk about personal qualities beyond what was required—through soft skills and “intrinsics.”
