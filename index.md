# ConfusedChairman
I’m ConfusedChairman, a 1st year at OSU studying Data Science. From a young
age, I’ve been interested in how data can inform important decisions. Through
being my FIRST Robotics team’s scouting lead, I helped collect, analyze, and
present data to my team. While participating in local government, I advocated for
accurate demographic data collection. Through these experiences I’ve seen how
data impacts the spaces I enter, and it has empowered me to explore Data
Science as a field. Over time I’ve developed a background in Computer Science,
which has helped me improve my problem-solving skills. I also attained a Data
Science Associate Certification from Data Camp and participated in a Wharton
High School Data Science competition, which improved my collaboration and
communication skills. Beyond these experiences, I hope to expand my
understanding of Data Science and its related fields! In particular, I’m fascinated
by quantitative investment, data pipelines, and predictive modeling. As I embark
on my college journey, I hope you’ll check back to see my progress!

## Career Goals
A career goal that I have is exploring the financial industry as it pertains to data science. I have been exposed to jobs within the finance industry on multiple occasions, but throughout the summer I grew more interested in how it related to predictive modeling and the utilization of increasingly large datasets. I have also started to wonder what a business side of tech job would look like as opposed to a tech side of business job.With prior experience in local government and exposure to economics through previous classes, I think this can be a great opportunity to apply data science to a specific niche. Of the key job titles that appealed to me–quantitative research, business analytics, and quantitative trading–I noted a few key features. Through doing an analysis of job postings (courtesy of my DS 101 class), I was able to identify communication, experience with high-dimensional datasets, and proficiency in SQL and Python as key skills. Based on this I have identified a few milestones that can shape my exploration. On a shorter-term basis, I want to complete an exploratory course, either through my schooling or personally, that focuses on business analytics and/or SQL. In addition, I want to complete a basic project involving predictive modeling for my portfolio. On a medium-term basis, I want to engage with professionals already in the field, whether that be through club networking or cold-emailing. I have already spoken with someone who is on the tech side of business, and it was an invaluable experience in gaining exposure and insight on potential jobs. I also plan on engaging with clubs related to this field, whether that be increasing my involvement in clubs I am already a member of or exploring new ones. Longer term, I want to attain an internship related to finance and business, and I think this will be greatly aided by my courses, projects, networking, and club involvement. Finance is an industry I am interested in, and I think that through these steps I will be able to gain further clarity on what a related job and career would look like.
